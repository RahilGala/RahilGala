# 💫 About Me:
I work in data analytics, focusing on cleaning messy datasets, finding patterns that matter and turning them into clear insights. I use SQL, Python and Power BI in most of my work, and I enjoy taking a raw dataset and shaping it into something that tells a story a business can act on. My projects mainly revolve around exploratory analysis, dashboard building and solving practical decision-making problems with data.
